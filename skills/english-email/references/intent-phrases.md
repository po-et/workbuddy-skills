# 七种意图：结构与可替换句式

回到 [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)。方括号处换成你的内容；不知道的事实用 `[待补：…]` 占位，不编。

## 主表

| 意图 | 结构 | 可替换句式（方括号处替换） |
|---|---|---|
| 请求 | 目的 → 理由 → 要什么 → 期限 | I'm writing to ask whether you could [send the Q3 figures] by [date]. / Would it be possible to [move the deadline to Friday]? |
| 跟进 | 指明上次哪封 → 还缺什么 → 新期限 → 给台阶 | Following up on my email of [date] about [topic]. / In case it got buried, here's a quick recap: [one line]. |
| 拒绝 | 感谢 → 明确说不 → 一句理由 → 替代方案 | Thank you for thinking of us. Unfortunately, we are unable to [accept the revised terms] at this time. / What we can offer instead is [alternative]. |
| 道歉 | 错在哪 → 影响 → 已补救 → 防再犯 | I'm sorry for [the incorrect invoice]. / We have [reissued it] and [added a second check] so it won't happen again. |
| 催款 | 发票号、金额、到期日 → 附件 → 问付款日期 | Our records show that invoice [number] for [amount], due on [date], remains unpaid. / Could you let us know when we can expect payment? |
| 约会议 | 目的 → 时长 → 两三个候选时间带时区 → 形式 | Would you be available for a [30-minute] call to discuss [topic]? / I'm free [Tue 9–10 am] or [Wed 3–4 pm], New York time. |
| 自我介绍 | 我是谁 → 怎么认识 → 为什么找你 → 低门槛请求 | [Name] suggested I reach out to you about [topic]. / Would you be open to a 15-minute call next week? |

## 每种意图再备一两句

- 请求：Would you be able to [share the draft] by [date]? / It would help us a lot to have [the figures] before [date].
- 跟进：Just checking in on [topic]. Could you let me know by [date] whether [question]?
- 拒绝：After careful consideration, we have decided not to [proceed with the proposal]. / We'd be glad to revisit this [next quarter].
- 道歉：Please accept our apologies for [the delay]. ——整封只道歉这一次，后面讲补救。
- 催款：Please let us know if there is any issue with the invoice that is holding up payment.
- 约会议：If none of these times work, please suggest a slot that suits you.
- 自我介绍：I'm [role] at [Company], where I [one line about what you do].

## 催款三级

| 级别 | 语气 | 做法 | 可替换句式 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 第一封 | 中性 | 发票号、金额、到期日、附件，问付款日期 | Our records show that invoice [number] … remains unpaid. Could you let us know when we can expect payment? |
| 第二封 | 正式 | 指明上次哪天那封，抄送对方财务 | I'm following up on my email of [date] regarding invoice [number], which is now [N] days overdue. |
| 第三封 | 正式 | 写明后续安排——措施以合同和公司政策为准 | Unless we receive payment by [date], we will [next step agreed in the contract / under our policy]. |

写到要采取法律手段那一步，先问法务，不在邮件里自行定性或威胁。

## 语气三档怎么切

同一句请求，三档写法：

- 正式：I would be grateful if you could confirm the delivery date by Friday, 15 May.
- 中性：Could you please confirm the delivery date by Friday, 15 May?
- 亲近：Can you confirm the delivery date by Friday?
